Riftbinder

A free collection tracker and deck builder for Riftbound players.

The app answers one question: which cards am I still missing to build my decks? Record how many copies of each card you own, build decklists, and see per deck which cards and how many you still need.

What it does

  • Library: browse all cards with search and filters (set, domain, type, rarity, energy, power, might) and set how many copies you own.
  • Decks: build decks (legend, champion, main deck, battlefields, runes, sideboard) or import a Riftbound deck code.
  • Missing: see for every deck what you still need, with all decks sharing your copies or each deck checked on its own.
  • Extras: deck statistics, a read-only record of a friend's collection to spot trades, and backup export/import.

It is a deck builder and card library only. It does not simulate or automate gameplay.

Free and private
