Riftbinder

A free collection tracker and deck builder for Riftbound players.

The app answers one question: which cards am I still missing to build my decks? Keep track of the cards you own, build decklists, and see for every deck exactly which cards and how many you still need.

What it does

Library

  • Browse every card as a list or a grid. Pinch to resize the grid.
  • Set how many copies of each card you own, right in the list, with undo.
  • Search by name, card code, tag or card text.
  • Filter by set, domain, type, rarity, variant, energy, power and might.
  • Tap a card to zoom in and swipe through the list.
  • "Hide Decks" shows only the copies that are not committed to a deck.

Decks

  • Build decklists with legend, champion, main deck, battlefields, runes and sideboard.
  • Import a deck from a Riftbound deck code, or copy your deck as a code to share it.
  • Drag decks to reorder them, long-press to duplicate, swipe to delete.
  • Edits stay in a draft until you save, and the app asks before you leave with unsaved changes.
  • Deck statistics: energy, might and power curves, plus type and domain mix.

Missing

  • For every deck, see which cards you still need and how many.
  • Tap a card to mark a copy as acquired.
  • "Shared copies" decides whether all decks may use the same copies of a card, or whether a copy belongs to one deck only.

Friend collections

  • Add another player's collection from a collection code. It is read-only.
  • See which of their cards you are missing, and record trades with an undo.
  • Share your own collection as a code, with or without your decks.

Settings and backup

  • Card size, tap sounds and volume.
  • Export your collection and decks to a backup file and restore them later, for example on a new phone.

It is a deck builder and card library only. It does not simulate or automate gameplay.

Free and private

  • Everything is stored on your device. No accounts, no server, no analytics.
