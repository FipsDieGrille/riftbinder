Riftbinder

A free collection tracker and deck builder for Riftbound players.

Riftbinder keeps your Riftbound collection and decks in one place. Track which cards you own, build and import decklists, see exactly which cards each deck is still missing, compare collections with friends to spot trades, and back everything up. It all stays on your device, with no account.

  • Free
  • No ads
  • No account
  • Data stays on your device

Library

Every card, and how many you own.

  • Browse as a list or a grid, and pinch to resize the grid.
  • Set how many copies you own right in the list, with undo.
  • Search by name, card code, tag or card text.
  • Filter by set, domain, type, rarity, variant, energy, power and might.
  • Tap a card to zoom in and swipe through the list.
  • "Hide Decks" shows only the copies that are not committed to a deck.
  • Add a friend's collection from a collection code. It is read-only.
  • See which of their cards you are missing, and record trades with an undo.

Decks

Build it, import it, share it.

  • Legend, champion, main deck, battlefields, runes and sideboard.
  • Import a deck from a Riftbound deck code, or copy your deck as a code to share it.
  • Drag decks to reorder them, long-press to duplicate, swipe to delete.
  • Edits stay in a draft until you save, and the app asks before you leave with unsaved changes.

Know what your decks need

The question the app was built to answer: what am I still missing?

  • Missing cards: for every deck, see which cards you still need and how many, and tap a card to mark a copy as acquired.
  • Shared copies: decide whether all decks may use the same copies of a card, or whether a copy belongs to one deck only.
  • Deck statistics: energy, might and power curves, plus the type and domain mix.

Settings and backup

Your collection is yours.

  • Share your own collection as a code, with or without your decks.
  • Export everything to a backup file and restore it later, for example on a new phone.
  • Choose the card size, and switch tap sounds and volume.
