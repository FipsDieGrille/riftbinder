Privacy Policy

Riftbinder
Last updated: 2026-10-25

Short version

The app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your card collection, decks, friend collections and settings are saved locally on your device. We (the developer) never receive this data. If you delete the app, the data is deleted with it, unless you have exported a backup file yourself.

What the app does not do

  • No account or login.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No collection of your name, email, location, contacts or device identifiers.

Network use

The app loads card images (and card data updates) from Riot Games' servers. Like any request to a web server, this transfers your device's IP address to that server. We do not control or receive that information. See Riot Games' privacy notice.

Files and clipboard

  • Backups: if you choose "Export Backup", the app creates a file and opens your device's share sheet. Where you send it is up to you. "Import Backup" reads a file you pick.
  • Collection codes: "Copy Code" places a text code on your clipboard when you tap it. The app does not read your clipboard on its own.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
